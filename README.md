# business-strategy-and-management
Collection of business strategy, management, and data analytics case studies for leading companies including Personal effectiveness, Adidas, IBM, Apple, and Zara. Projects include strategic analysis, CX strategy, data-driven insights, and recommendations.

# Business Strategy and Management Projects

Overview:
This repository contains five detailed business and data analysis projects. Each project includes methodology, data analysis, visualizations, and strategic recommendations for real-world companies.

⸻

## Projects Included:
	1.	Private Equity Case Study
	•	Analysis of investment opportunities, growth strategies, and financial modeling.
	•	Focus: Market analysis, risk assessment, and ROI evaluation.
	2.	Adidas Global Strategy Analysis
	•	Assessment of Adidas’ market positioning, supply chain, and competitive strategy.
	•	Tools: SWOT, PESTLE, and market research insights.
	3.	IBM Customer Experience and Data Analytics
	•	Analysis of IBM’s customer experience strategy and CX metrics.
	•	Focus: Consumer personas, journey mapping, omnichannel marketing, and loyalty strategies.
	4.	Apple MacBook CX Strategy
	•	Detailed study of Apple’s customer experience approach for MacBook.
	•	Includes persona creation, journey mapping, performance metrics (NPS, LTV), and CX insights.
	5.	Zara Global Expansion and Operations
	•	Examination of Zara’s international strategy, vertical integration, and supply chain management.
	•	Tools: PESTLE, SWOT, and case study on global operations.

⸻

## Tools & Techniques Used:
	•	Excel, Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn) for data analysis
	•	SWOT and PESTLE frameworks
	•	CX mapping and customer persona creation
	•	Data visualization and strategic reporting
